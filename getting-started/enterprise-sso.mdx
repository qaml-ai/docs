---
title: 'Enterprise SSO'
description: 'Connect your camelAI organization to Google Workspace or another OpenID Connect provider.'
---

Enterprise SSO lets people sign in to a camelAI organization through your
identity provider. camelAI supports OpenID Connect (OIDC), including Google
Workspace.

SSO is available on the Enterprise plan. Organization owners and admins can
configure and manage it.

## Before you start

You need:

- Permission to create an OAuth or OIDC client in your identity provider
- Owner or admin access to your camelAI organization
- A regular user account in your identity provider for testing

<Warning>
  Use a regular member account for the connection test. camelAI platform
  superusers cannot sign in through organization SSO.
</Warning>

## Choose who can join

The **Allow uninvited SSO users** setting controls whether an identity provider
login can create a member without a camelAI invitation.

| Setting | What happens on the first SSO sign-in |
| --- | --- |
| Off | Existing organization members and users with an active invitation can sign in. An invited user is created automatically with the role and workspace access selected in the invitation. |
| On | Any user allowed by the identity provider and camelAI domain rules can join. camelAI creates them as a regular member with access to the first active workspace. |

Leaving this setting off is the more controlled option. Invite each person from
your organization Team settings, then send them the SSO sign-in URL. They do not
need to create a password-based camelAI account or accept the invitation link
first. Their verified SSO login consumes the invitation.

## Set up Google Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the camelAI callback URL">
    In camelAI, open **Settings → Organization → Single sign-on** and copy the
    callback URL shown on the page.

    Use the displayed value exactly. It normally looks like:

    ```text
    https://camelai.dev/api/auth/enterprise-oidc/callback
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the Google OAuth consent screen">
    Open the [Google Auth Platform](https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/overview)
    in a Google Cloud project owned by your organization.

    Set the app audience to **Internal** so only users in your Google Workspace
    organization can authorize it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a web OAuth client">
    Create an OAuth client with the application type **Web application**.

    Add the camelAI callback URL under **Authorized redirect URIs**. You can
    leave **Authorized JavaScript origins** blank.

    Google may take a few minutes to apply a newly added redirect URI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the connection in camelAI">
    Copy the client ID and client secret into camelAI, then use these values:

    | camelAI field | Google Workspace value |
    | --- | --- |
    | Issuer URL | `https://accounts.google.com` |
    | Token endpoint authentication | **Client secret POST** |
    | Email claim | **email** |
    | Allowed email domains | Leave blank for the first test, or enter your exact Workspace domain |

    camelAI requests the `openid`, `email`, and `profile` scopes. It does not
    request access to Gmail, Drive, or other Workspace data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test connection** and complete the Google login with a regular
    Workspace user.

    camelAI checks the discovery document, authorization and token endpoints,
    signing keys, token exchange, verified email, and Google Workspace domain.
    The test does not create a user, link an account, or enable SSO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable SSO">
    When every connection check passes, click **Enable SSO**.

    camelAI shows an organization-specific sign-in URL. Test it in a private
    browser window before sharing it with your team or adding it to your
    internal app launcher.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set up another OIDC provider

Your provider must publish a standard discovery document from:

```text
<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration
```

The document must include HTTPS authorization, token, and JWKS endpoints.
Create a web application in your provider, register the callback URL shown in
camelAI, and configure it to return a stable subject identifier and email
claim.

Enter the issuer URL, client credentials, token endpoint authentication method,
and email claim in camelAI. camelAI discovers the remaining endpoints
automatically.

<Note>
  The issuer URL is the provider's exact issuer value, not the discovery
  document URL. For example, enter `https://accounts.google.com`, not a URL
  ending in `/.well-known/openid-configuration`.
</Note>

## Allowed email domains

Allowed domains are an additional camelAI-side boundary. They protect the
organization if the identity provider application is accidentally assigned too
broadly or its policy changes later.

Enter domains without `@`, for example:

```text
example.com
```

For Google Workspace, camelAI also requires a verified email and a matching
Google hosted-domain (`hd`) claim.

## Add and remove users

### Add a user

If uninvited users are not allowed:

1. Invite the exact email address from your camelAI Team settings.
2. Choose the user's organization role and workspace access.
3. Send the user your organization-specific SSO sign-in URL.

The first successful SSO login creates the account, consumes the invitation,
applies its access, and signs the user in.

If uninvited users are allowed, permit the user in your identity provider and
send them the same SSO sign-in URL. No camelAI invitation is required.

### Remove a user

Remove the member from the camelAI organization and disable or unassign them in
your identity provider. Removing the camelAI membership revokes that
organization's SSO access even if the provider still authenticates the user.

To restore a removed user, send a new invitation to the exact same email. Their
next SSO login restores the membership with the new invitation's role and
workspace access.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Could not validate the OIDC issuer and discovery document">
    Confirm that the issuer is an HTTPS URL and matches the provider's issuer
    exactly. Do not paste the discovery document URL itself.

    Open `<issuer>/.well-known/openid-configuration` in a browser and confirm it
    returns JSON. Its issuer must exactly match the value entered in camelAI,
    and its authorization, token, and JWKS endpoints must be valid HTTPS URLs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google reports redirect_uri_mismatch">
    Confirm that the callback URL shown in camelAI appears exactly under
    **Authorized redirect URIs** on the correct Google **Web application**
    client. Check the scheme, hostname, path, and trailing slash.

    If you just added the URI, wait a few minutes and try again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No organization member or active invitation matches this enterprise identity">
    Uninvited SSO users are not allowed, and camelAI could not find an active
    invitation for the exact email returned by the provider.

    Send a new invitation to that email, or turn on **Allow uninvited SSO
    users** if everyone permitted by the identity provider should be able to
    join.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="This email is already bound to a different enterprise identity">
    The email was previously linked to a different provider subject or SSO
    connection. camelAI blocks automatic rebinding to protect the existing
    account. Sign in normally and use the explicit account-link flow, or
    contact [support@camelai.com](mailto:support@camelai.com).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google Workspace did not verify this email and hosted domain">
    Use a managed Google Workspace account, confirm its email is verified, and
    check that the Google hosted domain matches your camelAI allowed-domain
    configuration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security notes

- camelAI binds each login to the provider's stable OIDC subject, not email
  alone.
- The provider must return the configured email claim. Google Workspace logins
  must also include a verified email and matching hosted domain.
- Invitation-gated account creation requires an active invitation for the exact
  asserted email.
- New SSO-created accounts are scoped to the organization and cannot become
  camelAI platform superusers.
- SSO sessions are restricted to the organization that issued them.
- Client secrets are encrypted at rest and are never shown again after saving.
