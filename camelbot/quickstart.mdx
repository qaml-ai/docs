---
title: 'Quickstart'
description: 'Build your first Discord bot with camelBot, try it, publish it, and add it to your server'
---

In a few minutes you will have a bot that camel-builder wrote for you, tried in
a private test channel, and running in your Discord server.

You need a Discord account, and the **Manage Server** permission in the server
you want to add the bot to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in with Discord">
    Open [bots.camelai.com](https://bots.camelai.com) and select **Sign in with
    Discord**. Discord asks to share your username, your avatar, and the list
    of servers you're in. Signing in creates your camelBot account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what your bot should do">
    The first screen asks **What should your bot do?** Describe it in your own
    words, the way you would explain it to a friend. For example:

    ```text
    Post a trivia question when asked, with answer buttons, and keep score
    ```

    You can change anything later. Select **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how your bot appears in Discord">
    Pick one of two options:

    - **Camel's app** (Recommended). Replies as camel, with commands like
      `/mochi roll`. Ready now, with nothing to set up.
    - **Your own app**. Replies with its own name and picture, with commands
      like `/roll`. It takes about 5 minutes on Discord's developer site.

    **See every difference** compares the two side by side. What the bot can
    do is the same either way, and you can switch later. For your first bot,
    Camel's app is the quickest. If you pick your own app, the next step is
    **Connect your Discord app**. See [Your own Discord
    app](/camelbot/own-app#connect-your-own-app) for that step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your bot">
    camelBot suggests a name and a picture. Change them if you like.

    On Camel's app the name is also the bot's command and how people talk to
    it, so it uses lowercase letters, numbers, `-` or `_`, with 32 characters
    or fewer. The names `camel`, `help` and `settings` are taken. The picture
    shows in camelBot only. In Discord, the bot's replies come from camel.

    Select **Create** followed by the name, such as **Create quizzy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it in #try-{name}">
    The chat with camel-builder opens, with your bot's test channel beside it.
    camel-builder starts from what you described. It writes the bot, checks
    it, tries it, and tells you how to use it. **camel-builder is working…**
    shows under the message box until it's done, with **Stop** to end it.

    Then try the bot yourself in `#try-quizzy`:

    - Type `/` to see its commands, then pick one.
    - Start a message with its name, such as `quizzy, hi`.
    - Press the buttons on its replies.

    Only you see this channel, and nothing goes to Discord. To change
    something, tell camel-builder in the chat, such as "make the questions
    harder", then try again. See [The test channel](/camelbot/test-channel)
    for everything you can try there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    After camel-builder saves a change, the chat shows a card that asks
    **Publish @quizzy as version 1?** Select **Publish v1**.

    You can also ask camel-builder to publish it. It shows a card with what
    will happen, and nothing is published until you select **Publish**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to Discord">
    Once the bot is published, the chat asks **@quizzy is published. Add it to
    Discord?** Select **Add to Discord**.

    Discord's own screen opens in a new tab. Pick the server and the channel
    there, and approve. The card in the chat shows **Waiting for Discord…**
    until the bot is in the channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use it in Discord

Open the channel you picked. On Camel's app, members can:

- Run its commands, such as `/quizzy trivia`.
- Start a message with its name, such as `quizzy, explain the rules`.
- Reply to one of its messages.

The bot's replies come from camel, with Discord's APP tag. In the channel you
added it to, the bot sees every message, so it can also answer messages that
don't use its name if you built it to. See [Which messages a bot
sees](/camelbot/limits#which-messages-a-bot-sees).
