camelBot is camelAI's [Discord bot builder](https://camelai.com/discord).
You say what a bot should do, and
camel-builder, camelBot's assistant, writes the bot and checks it. You try the
bot in a private test channel, then publish it and add it to your server.
camelBot runs the bot for you, so there is no code to write and no server to
keep online.

## What a bot can do

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Slash commands" icon="square-slash">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Buttons, menus, and forms" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    Replies can carry buttons and dropdown menus, and a command can open a

  </Card>

    conversation, make and edit images, and transcribe voice messages.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Saved data" icon="database">
    Scores, inventories, sign-ups, and settings stay saved between messages.
    You can download a copy at any time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedules and reminders" icon="alarm-clock">
    A post every morning, a weekly reminder, or a timer that someone sets
    with a command.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data from other sites" icon="globe">
    The bot can call public websites and APIs to check a game server, a
    price, or the weather.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Moderation and roles" icon="shield-check">
    Time out, kick, and ban members, give and take roles, and keep a case
    log, with the Discord permissions those actions need.

Features that manage members, roles, or channels need the matching Discord
permissions. Member welcomes also need Discord's Server Members intent.
See [Permissions and limits](/camelbot/limits) for what each feature needs
and which actions a bot can take.

## Bot ideas

If you're not sure what to build, start from one of these ideas.

- **Run your server.** A [Discord moderation
  bot](https://camelai.com/discord/moderation-bot), a [ticket
  bot](https://camelai.com/discord/ticket-bot), a [welcome
  bot](https://camelai.com/discord/welcome-bot), or a [verification
  bot](https://camelai.com/discord/verification-bot).
- **Talk, celebrate, and play.** A [voice bot that talks
  back](https://camelai.com/discord/voice-bot), a [translator
  bot](https://camelai.com/discord/translator-bot), a [birthday
  bot](https://camelai.com/discord/birthday-bot), or an [RPG
  bot](https://camelai.com/discord/rpg-bot).

See more [Discord bots you can make](https://camelai.com/discord/bots).

## How it works

1. **Describe the bot.** Sign in with Discord and answer "What should your bot
   do?" in your own words.
2. **camel-builder writes it.** It writes the bot's code, checks it, tries
   it, and tells you how to use it.
3. **Try it in the test channel.** Each bot has a private channel,
   `#try-{name}`, beside the chat. You message the bot, run its commands, and
   press its buttons there. Nothing goes to Discord.
4. **Publish it.** When the bot works the way you want, you publish it.
   camel-builder never publishes on its own.
5. **Add it to a server.** Discord's own screen picks the server and the
   channel, and the bot is live.

To change a live bot later, describe the change to camel-builder, try it in
the test channel, and publish again. Every publish is a version you can roll
back to, and the bot's saved data stays.

## How your bot appears in Discord

Every bot starts in one of two ways:

- **Camel's app.** Nothing to set up. It can use your bot's name and picture
  in a server, where all bots on the shared app use the first-added profiled
  bot's look. Commands start with your bot's name, like `/mochi roll`.
- **Your own app.** The bot replies with its own name and picture, and its
  commands are its own, like `/roll`. Setup takes about 5 minutes on Discord's
  developer site.

Both support the same bot features, subject to Discord permissions and
intents, and you can switch later. See [Your own Discord app](/camelbot/own-app).

## Cost

camelBot is free while in beta. AI that your bots use, such as chat replies,
images, and voice transcription, draws on a daily allowance for each bot.
camel-builder's
own work and AI in your tests draw on a separate daily allowance for your
account. Both reset at midnight UTC. See [AI
allowances](/camelbot/limits#ai-allowances).
