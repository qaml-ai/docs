camelStream doesn't sell a model. It sells a floor. Your requests are served
by a changing fleet of frontier models, and every model must clear the same
intelligence floor before it can serve a stream. This page is the official,
current list.

## The intelligence floor

Every model in the fleet scores at or above **70% on
[Terminal-Bench 2.1](https://www.tbench.ai/leaderboard/terminal-bench/2.1)**
or **50 on the
[Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index](https://artificialanalysis.ai/evaluations/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index)**.
Below the bar, a model doesn't serve.

## What every stream is guaranteed

- **The intelligence floor.** Every fleet model scores at or above 70% on
  Terminal-Bench 2.1 or 50 on the AA Intelligence Index.
- **Latest versions.** When your stream is paired with a model, you get its
  most recent publicly available version, not a frozen snapshot.
- **260K context.** Every request gets at least a 260K-token window, and more
  when the model serving you supports it.
- **One generation per stream, always.** Bursts of extra parallelism can
  happen when capacity allows, but only streams guarantee it.

## Context beyond the guarantee

When a conversation grows past what the serving model supports, camelStream
compacts the middle of the conversation first. Older messages are hollowed
out while their envelopes stay in place, so roles and tool-call IDs keep
lining up, and your original task and the latest turns stay intact.

## Speed targets

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| **Throughput** | p10 at or above 40 tokens per second, p5 at or above 20 tokens per second |
| **Time to first token** | p95 under 5 seconds |

Throughput percentiles are floors: p10 at or above 40 tok/s means 90% of
requests stream at 40 tok/s or faster. The first-token percentile is a
ceiling: 95% of requests see a first token within 5 seconds. Targets are
tuning goals, not a service-level agreement. See the
[camelStream Terms](https://camelai.com/stream/terms).

## How the flat price works

camelStream sources capacity across the fleet and partner providers, wherever
frontier-floor intelligence is most economical: bulk deals, new-model offers,
and data-for-training arrangements. That sourcing is what subsidizes
unlimited tokens at a flat price. Requests and responses may be retained and
used to train AI models, by us or by our inference providers. Your account
details never are. The [camelStream Terms](https://camelai.com/stream/terms)
and [Privacy Policy](https://camelai.com/stream/privacy) describe the data
rights in full.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What model am I getting?">
    A model from the fleet above. camelStream pairs your stream with a fleet
    model, and pairing can change between sessions. We don't guarantee which
    model serves a given request. What is guaranteed is the floor: every
    fleet model scores at or above 70% on Terminal-Bench 2.1 or 50 on the AA
    Intelligence Index, and you always get the model's most recent publicly
    available version.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to update the deepseek-v4-flash model ID in my config?">
    Eventually, yes. The model ID is now `auto`, which means your request is
    served by whatever fleet model is available, and every guide in these
    docs configures it. Configs still sending the legacy `deepseek-v4-flash`
    ID keep working: until September 1, 2026 those requests are still served
    by DeepSeek V4 Flash, and after that the legacy ID is treated the same
    as `auto`. We recommend you switch to `auto` now so the change is behind
    you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do you use my prompts for training?">
    Requests and responses may be retained and used to train AI models, by
    us or by our inference providers. Your account details never are. These
    arrangements are part of what keeps the price flat. The
    [camelStream Terms](https://camelai.com/stream/terms) and
    [Privacy Policy](https://camelai.com/stream/privacy) are the full
    description.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pin one specific model or version?">
    Not on a standard subscription. Streams are served by the fleet, and the
    fleet changes. If your workload needs one pinned model or version, we
    recommend you ask about a custom agreement at
    [camelai.com/stream/contact](https://camelai.com/stream/contact).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
